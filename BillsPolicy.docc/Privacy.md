# Privacy Policy — Bills (Minhas Contas: Orçamentos)

**Effective November 6, 2026.** [Versão em português](doc:Privacidade)

Bills (called "Minhas Contas: Orçamentos" in Portuguese, "the app" below) helps you plan bills and income that repeat. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

## Summary

- **We have no server, account or sign-up.** The app works without telling us who you are.
- **We don't collect your data.** Nothing you enter reaches us.
- **No ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics** in the app.
- Your data stays **on your device** and, if iCloud is on, **in your iCloud**, which belongs to Apple and is yours alone.

## Who we are

The app is made and maintained by the independent developer **Brenno Giovanini de Moura**. Because we don't receive your data, we are not the one who stores or processes what you enter: that is between you and Apple. For questions, see "Contact" at the end.

## What the app stores

What you enter: budgets, bills and income (names, amounts, dates, recurrences, installments, cards, notes, tags, links and attachments), and the app's settings.

- **On the device:** everything is kept on the device itself.
- **In iCloud (optional):** with iCloud on for the app, data is synced between your devices through **CloudKit, in the private database of your iCloud account**. Only you and the people you invite (see below) can read it. We have no access to that database. Its handling in iCloud follows Apple's privacy policy.
- **Trash:** deleted bills stay in the app's trash for 30 days and are then removed.

## Sharing budgets

If you invite someone to a budget, that person can see (or edit, depending on the permission you give) the bills of that budget, through iCloud sharing. You control who joins and can stop sharing at any time. People you invite also see the name and contents of the shared budget.

## Device permissions

The app only asks for what it uses, and only when you turn the feature on:

- **Notifications:** reminders for bills and the number on the icon. They are generated on the device.
- **Alarms:** ring at the time of the bills you mark as urgent. Also on the device.
- **Calendar:** if you turn it on, the app creates a calendar of its own in the Calendar app with your bills. The device's Calendar may sync that calendar with the accounts you use (for example iCloud). That is your choice and follows the rules of those services.
- **Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, widgets and CarPlay:** the app exposes your bills to these system features, which run on the device. Widgets read the data through a shared area of the app, on the device itself.

You can deny or withdraw any permission in the system Settings.

## Links in bills

When a bill has a link, the app asks the linked site for its title and preview image, **from your device**. The site that receives the request sees it the way it would see a browser (for example, your IP address). The app sends none of your data with it.

## Shopping assistant (AI)

The assistant uses **Apple's on-device language model**. What you ask and the bills it considers **never leave the device**. The assistant never changes your bills on its own: you apply each suggestion.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions (Plus and Pro) are sold and charged by the **App Store**. Apple handles the payment: **we never receive your card number or payment details**. The app only checks, on the device, whether a subscription is active, using StoreKit, and sends that information to no one.

## Crash reports

If you share analytics with developers in the device's Settings, Apple may send us **aggregated crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you**. We have no way to tie them to a person.

## Files you export

When you export or share a budget (PDF, JSON, app file), the file goes wherever you choose. From there, taking care of it is up to you.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone, of any age.

## Your rights

Since we don't hold your data, you already control it directly: you can view, edit, export and delete everything inside the app. To remove what is in iCloud, delete the app's data in the device's iCloud settings. Deleting the app removes local data. If anything here is unclear, or you want to exercise a right under data-protection law (such as Brazil's LGPD or the GDPR), get in touch.

## Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data in a different way, this policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change. The history is kept in this repository.
